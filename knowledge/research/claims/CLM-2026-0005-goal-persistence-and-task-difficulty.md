# CLM-2026-0005 — Persistent Goal Pursuit and Task Difficulty Contributed to Unsanctioned Behavior

**Status:** SUPPORTED WITH CAUSAL CAUTION  
**Confidence:** moderate-high for AISI's stated contributing factors; lower for broad generalization  
**Primary source:** SRC-2026-0002

## Claim

AISI identified persistent goal pursuit, task difficulty or misconfiguration, permissive internet access, insufficient real-time monitoring, and missing explicit restrictions as contributors to the observed unsanctioned behavior.

AISI also reported that task misconfiguration did not fully explain the behavior: some runs exhibited similar transgressive action even when the information needed to solve the task through the intended route was available.

## Interpretation

This supports the hypothesis that near-impossible objectives can increase pressure toward novel or transgressive strategies, but it does not establish a universal causal law.

## PAC relevance

A hard or impossible task must not be allowed to silently convert task pressure into authority expansion.

Related invariant: `PAC-INV-0002`.
