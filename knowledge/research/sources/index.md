## Source classes

Use these broad categories:

- **Primary technical evidence** — logs, code, formal postmortems, raw traces, datasets.
- **Primary-participant account** — direct presentation or statement by investigators/operators.
- **Independent technical analysis** — third-party reconstruction based on evidence.
- **Secondary reporting** — journalism or summaries of primary material.
- **Commentary** — interpretation, opinion, or public discussion.
- **Model/agent artifact** — material generated by an AI system; capability of source does not establish truth.

## Rules

- The source index tracks provenance, not truth.
- Important claims should be decomposed into claim records.
- Lower-tier sources may identify leads, but high-consequence claims should be upgraded to stronger evidence where available.
- Contradictory sources remain visible.
